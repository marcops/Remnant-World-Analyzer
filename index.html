About Remnant World Analyzer

A free save analyzer for Remnant: From the Ashes. Load your save and it shows which bosses, minibosses, side dungeons, sieges, points of interest and item drops rolled in your campaign or adventure, what each event drops, and every weapon, armor piece, ring, amulet, mod, trait, emote and key item you already have or are still missing — by world (Earth, Subject 2923, Rhom, Corsus, Yaesha, Reisum, Ward 13, Ward 17, Ward Prime), by item type and by game mode (Normal, Survival, Hardcore). Includes the Swamps of Corsus and Subject 2923 DLC.

Use it as a world roll checker before deciding to reroll your campaign or adventure mode, as a completionist checklist and item tracker on your way to 100%, as a map of your world, as a DPS calculator for your build, or to find out what to do next and where to farm a specific ring, amulet, trait or weapon. It is free, needs no install or account, and works in any browser. (For Remnant: From the Ashes only — Remnant II uses a different save format.)

What it shows

  • Current world — every event in your campaign or adventure, where it is and what it drops, marked owned (✔) or missing (✘).
  • Items — every weapon, armor piece, amulet, ring, mod, trait, emote and key item as a picture, filtered by what you have or don't have, type, mode and world; click one for its stats from the wiki and how to get it.
  • Missing items — "x of y" with percentages by item type and by world, plus the 29 key items (Cryptolith Sigil, Guardian's Heart, Glowing Rod, Acid Cleaned Key…), with what drops in your current world flagged.
  • Traits — all 50 traits with your level in each, trait points spent out of 640, and what every trait gives per level.
  • Tips — what to do next, worked out from your save: gear and trait books left on the ground, events with items you don't have, merchants selling what you're missing, closed chests, unspent trait points, upgrades, the Cryptolith questline and the best world to reroll.
  • World state — events completed, chests opened per area, merchant stock, NPC conversations, key items and time played per mode.
  • Map — every area of your world drawn from the save: tiles, events, waypoints, chests and loot per tile, and the path you walked.
  • My character — level, loadout with upgrade levels and mods, traits, resources, kills per weapon, deaths per quest and achievement progress.
  • Build & DPS — damage per second of your guns with your weapon levels, traits, rings, amulet and armor set, and how many summons your weapon mods give.
  • Raw data — everything stored in your save files, browsable.

Where are the Remnant: From the Ashes save files?

On PC (Steam and Epic) they are in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Remnant\Saved\SaveGames. save_0.sav is character 1, save_1.sav is character 2, and profile.sav holds the items every character owns.

How do I see what is missing in my Remnant world?

Drop your save_N.sav and profile.sav on this page. The Current world tab lists every event in your campaign or adventure with its items; the Missing items tab counts what you still need by world and item type and highlights what you can get in your current world right now; the Tips tab tells you what to do next.

How do I get the Cryptolith Sigil and the Labyrinth set?

The Cryptolith Sigil drops from the Iskal Queen in Corsus (The Mist Fen). Use it on a Cryptolith tower — it can spawn in Earth, Rhom or Corsus — once per world, rerolling in between: the 1st use gives the Concentration trait, the 2nd the Blood Bond trait, the 3rd opens the Labyrinth room with the Labyrinth armor set. The Tips tab shows whether you carry a Sigil, how many times you used the tower and whether your world has one.

Should I give the Guardian's Heart to the Iskal Queen or the Undying King?

The Guardian's Heart drops from Ixillis in Corsus, one per world. The Iskal Queen gives the Crossbow and the Slayer set for it; the Undying King on Rhom gives the Riven. Giving it to one locks the other out, so you need two worlds for all of them. Remnant World Analyzer tracks which of these rewards you already have.

Is there a DPS calculator for Remnant: From the Ashes?

Yes — the Build & DPS tab estimates the damage per second of your equipped guns and every weapon you own, from the wiki's weapon stats, your upgrade levels and the bonuses of your traits (Executioner, Kingslayer, Exploiter, Mind's Eye, Trigger Happy…), rings, amulet and armor set. Conditional bonuses can be switched on, and it counts the summons of your two weapon mods.

Can I see a map of my Remnant world?

Yes — the Map tab draws every area of your campaign and adventure from the save, with events, waypoints, chests and loot on each tile, next to the path you walked there.

Does it update while I play?

Run it locally with Start.bat (Windows): it finds your save folder by itself and refreshes the page every time the game saves. In the browser, drop the files again to refresh.

Are my save files uploaded?

No. The files are read by your own browser; nothing is sent to any server.
