About Remnant World Analyzer

A free save checker for Remnant: From the Ashes. Load your save and it shows which bosses, minibosses, side dungeons, sieges, points of interest and item drops rolled in your campaign or adventure, what each event drops, and every weapon, armor piece, ring, amulet, mod, trait and emote you already have or are still missing — by world (Earth, Rhom, Corsus, Yaesha, Reisum, Ward 13, Ward 17, Ward Prime), by item type and by game mode (Normal, Survival, Hardcore). Includes the Swamps of Corsus and Subject 2923 DLC.

Use it as a world roll checker before deciding to reroll your campaign or adventure mode, as a completionist checklist to track your way to 100%, or to find out where to farm a specific ring, amulet, trait or weapon. It is free, needs no install or account, and works in any browser. (For Remnant: From the Ashes only — Remnant II uses a different save format.)

Where are the Remnant: From the Ashes save files?

On PC (Steam and Epic) they are in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Remnant\Saved\SaveGames. save_0.sav is character 1, save_1.sav is character 2, and profile.sav holds the items every character owns.

How do I see what is missing in my Remnant world?

Drop your save_N.sav and profile.sav on this page. The Current world tab lists every event in your campaign or adventure with its items; the Missing items tab counts what you still need by world, item type and mode, and highlights what you can get in your current world right now.

Are my save files uploaded?

No. The files are read by your own browser; nothing is sent to any server.
